WHO WE ARE

More Than Events.
We're an Experience.

ENCORE is the Event Management Committee of the ENTC Department at Trinity Academy of Engineering. We bring students together through creativity, collaboration, and unforgettable campus experiences.

From technical activities to cultural celebrations, we create opportunities for students to connect, discover their talents, and make memories together.
